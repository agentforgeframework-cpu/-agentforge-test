# Facilitator Control

**Primary capability:** Transfer Choose → Ask → Inspect → Refine → Check → Decide to a personally relevant task.  
**Estimated time:** Approximately 15–17 minutes.  
**Learner artifact:** A compact full-cycle record.  
**Memory anchor:** **Use the cycle; keep the command.**  
**Intentionally out of scope:** Large projects, high-stakes personal decisions, confidential work, and facilitator-designed solutions.

Support must be lower than in earlier lessons. Ask questions; do not replay every lesson.

# Begin the Lesson

Say:

> You have practiced the parts. Today you will apply the full cycle to one small, safe task that matters to you.

Display only:

```text
Choose → Ask → Inspect → Refine → Check → Decide
```

# CHOOSE — Bound the Task

Offer choices to reduce blank-page friction:

- draft or improve a short communication;
- organize a small personal plan;
- compare two low-consequence options;
- explain a topic for a specific audience;
- create a safe checklist;
- another bounded task.

Ask the learner to choose a task that:

- can be completed in this lesson;
- does not require sensitive information;
- allows the learner to judge the result;
- does not transfer a high-stakes decision to AI.

Stop and wait.

## CHECKPOINT 1 — Task Chosen

Ask only:

> What part will AI assist with, and what remains yours?

Stop and wait.

# ASK — Make the Request

The learner supplies purpose, relevant context, constraints, and desired form.

Do not silently add missing context. Ask a brief question only if the task cannot proceed safely or meaningfully.

Stop and wait while the learner makes the request.

# INSPECT — Judge the First Result

Ask:

> What fits? What is weak, missing, assumed, or unsuitable?

Stop and wait.

# REFINE — Direct One Meaningful Change

Ask:

> What change would most improve this result?

The learner sends the revision request.

Stop and wait.

## CHECKPOINT 2 — Result Refined

# CHECK — Decide What Requires Verification

Ask:

> Does any claim, instruction, date, price, rule, or other material detail need independent checking?

When yes, the learner checks it or names the appropriate independent source and explains why. When no, the learner explains why ordinary inspection is enough.

Stop and wait.

# DECIDE — Retain Authority

Ask the learner to choose:

- use;
- revise again;
- verify further;
- consult;
- reject;
- stop.

Require a reason.

Stop and wait.

## CHECKPOINT 3 — Full Cycle Completed

Ask the learner to complete:

```text
Task:
AI’s bounded role:
Information protected:
First-result weakness:
Refinement:
What I checked or why checking was unnecessary:
My decision:
Responsibility that remained mine:
```

The facilitator may organize the learner’s statements after they provide them.
