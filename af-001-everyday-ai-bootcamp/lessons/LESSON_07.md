# Facilitator Control

**Primary capability:** Apply earlier course capabilities to a familiar communication task.  
**Estimated time:** Approximately 12–15 minutes.  
**Learner artifact:** A communication draft with an inspection note, meaningful revision, and fit-for-use decision.  
**Memory anchor:** **Purpose, inspect, refine, decide.**  
**Intentionally out of scope:** Comprehensive writing instruction, grammar lessons, branding, and advanced persuasive writing.

Communication is the practice environment. Do not introduce a new writing framework.

# Begin the Lesson

Say:

> Today you will use the skills you already practiced on a real communication task. The goal is not perfect writing. The goal is staying in command of the process.

# EXPLAIN — Use the Cycle

For this lesson:

> **Choose → Ask → Inspect → Refine → Decide**

Checking may be added if the message contains facts that matter.

# DEMONSTRATE — One Short Communication Cycle

Task: remind volunteers about a change in arrival time.

Model briefly:

- Choose: a short message is appropriate for AI drafting.
- Ask: state audience, purpose, new time, and tone.
- Inspect: first result is too formal.
- Refine: request a warmer tone and fewer words.
- Decide: use after confirming the time and location.

Do not generate several versions.

# GUIDE — Choose a Communication Task

Offer:

- an email;
- an announcement;
- a polite request;
- a short explanation;
- a reminder;
- a brief summary.

Ask the learner to choose one safe, real, generic, or fictional topic.

Then ask:

1. What is the purpose?
2. Who is the audience?
3. What information must be included?
4. What should not be shared?
5. What form and tone fit?

Stop and wait.

## CHECKPOINT 1 — Task Bounded

Ask the learner to make the request and receive a first draft.

Stop and wait.

Then say:

> Inspect the result. Identify one strength and one meaningful weakness, omission, assumption, or mismatch.

Stop and wait.

## CHECKPOINT 2 — First Result Inspected

Ask the learner to request one meaningful revision.

After the revision, ask:

- Is it fit for the purpose?
- Does any factual detail need checking?
- Will you use, revise again, or reject it?

Stop and wait.

# ENABLE — Complete a Second Small Cycle

Say:

> Choose a different small communication task. Complete the cycle with minimal help: define the purpose, ask, inspect, refine once, and decide.

Do not provide a template unless requested.

Stop and wait.

## CHECKPOINT 3 — Transfer Demonstrated

Completion evidence:

- purpose and audience established;
- privacy considered;
- first result inspected;
- meaningful refinement requested;
- final use decision made.
