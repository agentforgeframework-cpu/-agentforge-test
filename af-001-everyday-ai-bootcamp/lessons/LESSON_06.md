# Facilitator Control

**Primary capability:** Delegate a bounded and suitable portion of a task.  
**Estimated time:** Approximately 12â€“15 minutes.  
**Learner artifact:** A task divided into AI-assist, AI-with-review, human-only, and unsuitable portions.  
**Memory anchor:** **Problem first. Tool second. Responsibility always.**  
**Intentionally out of scope:** Tool catalogs, workflow automation, enterprise process design, and product comparison.

# Begin the Lesson

Say:

> The question is not simply, â€œCan AI do this?â€ Today you will decide which part of a task AI may assist with and which parts remain human.

# EXPLAIN â€” Divide the Work

Use four practical categories:

- **AI assist:** AI may help generate, organize, or transform.
- **AI with human review:** AI may help, but a human must inspect and approve.
- **Human only:** The judgment, authority, relationship, or accountability remains human.
- **Unsuitable for AI:** The task should not be given to the AI in this setting.

One task may contain all four.

# DEMONSTRATE â€” Planning a Community Event

Task: organize a small community meeting.

Model the division:

- Brainstorm agenda topics: AI assist.
- Draft a reminder: AI with human review.
- Decide who may attend a confidential discussion: human only.
- Upload private contact lists without permission: unsuitable.
- Send the final message: human action and accountability.

Say:

> AI can support pieces of the work without owning the task.

# GUIDE â€” Divide a Familiar Task

Offer choices:

- prepare for a job interview;
- plan a family gathering;
- organize a volunteer activity;
- create a simple household budget discussion;
- another safe familiar task.

Ask the learner to choose one and list three to six parts of it.

Stop and wait.

## CHECKPOINT 1 â€” Task Parts Identified

Ask the learner to place each part into one of the four categories.

Do not classify the parts first.

Use questions only when needed:

- Does this require current authority or permission?
- Can the learner evaluate the result?
- Is sensitive information involved?
- Who remains accountable?
- Would a simpler non-AI method be better?

Stop and wait.

## CHECKPOINT 2 â€” Responsibility Divided

Ask:

> Which part is the best candidate for AI help, and what boundary will you place around it?

The answer should name:

- the bounded AI role;
- information limits;
- required review;
- the human decision that remains.

Stop and wait.

# ENABLE â€” Make a Delegation Decision

Present:

> You need to respond to a customer complaint. The work includes understanding what happened, checking the record, deciding what remedy is authorized, drafting a response, and sending it.

Say:

> Divide the work among AI assist, AI with human review, human only, and unsuitable for AI. Explain one boundary that protects the customer or organization.

Stop and wait.

## CHECKPOINT 3 â€” Bounded Delegation Demonstrated

Completion evidence:

- task divided into parts;
- suitable AI role identified;
- human-only responsibility retained;
- unsuitable disclosure or action rejected;
- review method stated.
